# Helpline Staff — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8 October 2026

Helpline Staff is the internal attendance and daily-reporting app of Helpline Welfare Trust ("the Trust", "we"). It is used only by the Trust's employees, with accounts created by the Trust's administrators. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

| Information | Where it comes from | Why we use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, work email, phone number, employee code, designation and role | Entered by the Trust's administrator when your account is created | To identify you in the app and show your profile |
| Password | Set by the administrator | To sign you in. It is sent over an encrypted connection and is not stored on your device |
| Attendance records: the date and time of each check-in and check-out, and the project you checked in to | Created when you press Check in or Check out | To keep the Trust's attendance record |
| Daily reports: the summary and details you write, and the project they relate to | Typed by you | To record the work carried out on each project |
| Sign-in token | Created when you sign in | Kept in your device's secure storage so you stay signed in |
| Duty reminder (only if you allow notifications) | Created on your phone when you are on duty | To remind you at 7:30 PM to send your report before duty closes at 8 PM. Nothing is sent to the Trust |

The app does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Who can see your information

- You can see your own profile, attendance and reports.
- Supervisors can see the same-day attendance status and report summaries of staff assigned to their projects.
- The Trust's administrators can see all records through the Trust's admin dashboard.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing. Information is stored on servers operated for the Trust by its hosting and database providers, who process it only on the Trust's instructions. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

## Security

All communication between the app and the Trust's servers uses HTTPS encryption. Your sign-in token is stored in the device's encrypted keystore (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) and is removed when you log out.

## Retention and deletion

Attendance and report records are kept for as long as the Trust needs them for employment, administrative and legal purposes. Accounts are created and removed by the Trust's administrators. To ask for your account or personal information to be corrected or deleted, contact us using the details below; we will respond within 30 days. Records the Trust is legally required to keep may be retained after an account is closed.

## Children

The app is for the Trust's employees only and is not directed at children.
